Features

Instant transfers land in under 10 seconds, whether you send to M-Pesa, a bank, or another LipaHewa user. There is no pending screen and no extra trip to an agent.

Bank-level security sits on every transaction: PIN and biometric checks protect rent, supplier payouts, and small everyday sends alike.

Pricing

Zero hidden fees. You see the exact cost before you confirm — whether you are sending 50 shillings or 50,000. Get started free; you only pay when you move money.

Clear pricing means no surprise deductions at the destination. What you approve is what leaves your account.

FAQ

What is LipaHewa?

LipaHewa is a Kenyan payments app that lets you send money instantly to M-Pesa, bank accounts or other LipaHewa users.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You always see the exact cost before you send, whether it's 50 shillings or 50000.

How long do transfers take?

Most ransfers complete in under 10 seconds, whether you're sending to M-Pesa or another bank.

Is my money safe?

Every transaction requires PIN and biometric verification, and all transfers are encrypted end-to-end.

Can I use LipaHewa outside Kenya?

Currently LipaHewa only supports transfers within Kenya, with regional expansion planned for next year.
